## שאלות ותשובות

### מהו Past states and past actions?

'The pupils were worried' describes a past state. 'The bus stopped' describes a completed action. The verbs do different jobs in the account. Be uses was with I/he/she/it and were with you/we/they: 'The room was open'; 'The pupils were not alone'. A state can include a feeling or location: 'Amir was worried'; 'Amir was on the bus'. A past account can combine states, actions, and locations without using the same pattern for every verb.

### מהו Regular and irregular past forms?

Regular past verbs often end in -ed: travelled, checked, stopped. Irregular verbs have other past forms: leave becomes left, take becomes took, and reach is regular: reached. For a negative with an ordinary verb, did not carries the past meaning: 'The driver did not continue'; 'Amir did not lose his folder'. Continue and lose stay in the base form after did not. 'Did not went' would mark the past twice. The positive 'went' and the negative 'did not go' belong to the same verb but use different patterns.

### מהו The limit of a supported answer?

The text supports 'The road was closed' as a reason for the route change. It does not support 'The driver disliked the usual road'. That would add a motive the account never gives. A question may mention a possibility without proving it. 'Was your folder still with you?' asks about a possible state; Amir's answer confirms it. Before and after also clarify order: 'Before she continued, she checked another route' places the check first. A careful answer preserves both the stated event and the limits of what is known.

### An account says, 'The train arrived late, but the visitors entered before the doors closed.' Does the late arrival prove that they missed the exhibition?

The train's late arrival does not prove that the visitors missed the exhibition. The same sentence says they entered before the doors closed. Reading the whole account distinguishes arriving late from missing an event entirely; late alone cannot establish the outcome.

## How do past questions reveal a missing fact?

An account of a past event tells what happened, where people were, and what they did. Questions can uncover a missing fact, but a useful answer must stay within the available information. The journey, timeline, and interview below are fictional.

עזרה בעברית

תיאור של אירוע בעבר כולל גם פעולות וגם מצבים ומקומות. שאלה מבקשת להשלים מידע, אך התשובה צריכה להתבסס על מה שנמסר ולא על פרט שנשמע סביר בלבד.

## The Delayed Journey

On Friday, the school art group travelled to a small exhibition. Their bus left the school at nine. Noga sat beside the window, and Amir carried a folder of drawings. The group was excited because several pupils had pictures in the display. At half past nine, the bus stopped near a closed road. An accident, an unexpected harmful event, blocked the road earlier that morning. The driver did not continue along the same route. She spoke to a worker, checked another road, and explained the change to the group. The pupils were safe on the bus, but they were worried about missing the opening. The bus took a longer route and reached the exhibition at ten fifteen. The group missed the first short speech, but the doors were still open. A helper welcomed them and showed them their pictures. Amir did not lose his folder. He used the extra journey time to read the labels again. At the end of the visit, the pupils thanked the driver for explaining the delay clearly.

עזרה בעברית

הקבוצה משנה מסלול בגלל כביש סגור ומגיעה אחרי תחילת האירוע. הבחינו בין הסיבה לעיכוב לבין דברים שלא נמסרו, כמו פרטים על נפגעים בתאונה.

## Event and account words

accident / delay

An accident is an unexpected harmful event. A delay makes something happen later than expected. The account states that an accident closed a road; it does not describe injuries.

route / reached

A route is the way taken between places. Reached means arrived at: 'They reached the exhibition at ten fifteen.'

account / fact

An account tells about events. A fact in an account is information actually stated or clearly established there. A believable extra detail is not automatically a stated fact.

עזרה בעברית

״תאונה״ ו״עיכוב״ קשורים כאן, אך אינם אותה מילה: האחת היא האירוע והאחר הוא האיחור שנגרם. תיאור האירוע אינו נותן רשות להוסיף פרטים שלא נכתבו.

## The journey timeline

09:00: The bus left school. 09:30: The bus stopped near the closed road. After the stop: The driver checked a different route before she continued. 10:15: The bus reached the exhibition. Later: A helper showed the group their pictures. The timeline records only the details supplied in the account. It gives no exact time for the accident and no name for the worker.

## Questions about the past

Be as the main verb

'Was Amir worried?' 'Yes, he was.' 'Where were the pupils?' 'They were on the bus.' Put was/were before the subject to ask about a past state or location.

An ordinary action verb

'Did the bus leave at nine?' 'Yes, it did.' 'Why did the driver change the route?' 'The road was closed.' Did is followed by the base verb, even when the affirmative past form is irregular.

עזרה בעברית

בשאלה על מצב או מקום עם be מקדימים את was או were לנושא. בשאלה על פעולה רגילה בעבר משתמשים ב-did ואחריו פועל בצורת הבסיס.

## An eyewitness interview

Interviewer: Were you on the art-group bus? Amir: Yes, I was. Interviewer: When did the bus leave school? Amir: It left at nine. Interviewer: Did the driver use the usual road after the stop? Amir: No, she did not. She checked another route. Interviewer: What did you miss at the exhibition? Amir: We missed the first short speech. Interviewer: Was your folder still with you? Amir: Yes. I kept it with me and read the labels again.

עזרה בעברית

הריאיון בודק פרטים מתוך הנסיעה. בתשובה קצרה חוזרים לפועל העזר המתאים, ואפשר להוסיף משפט שמבהיר את האירוע או מתקן הנחה שבשאלה.

## Question words narrow the search

When asks for time, where for place, what for the action or thing, and why for the reason. Was/were and did are chosen according to the verb pattern. A correct form and an accurate fact must work together.

עזרה בעברית

מילת השאלה מכוונת את החיפוש: מתי, איפה, מה או למה. לאחר איתור המידע בדקו אם המשפט משתמש ב-was/were או ב-did לפי סוג הפועל.
