# Clockwork Alibi

Core verbs: walk, inspect, listen/read, scrub, reconstruct, replay, intervene, recover.

In sixty seconds: enter the miniature station, approach the porter, pause to read an English account, seek another viewpoint, scrub the loop, record an event inside an ongoing action, then replay with an intervention. The decision joins time, object identity and a physically useful action. Knowledge persists between loops. A wrong reconstruction produces an explicit contradiction; a mistimed intervention lets the object travel away and permits rewind/retry. Win by preserving/recovering the object after a supported reconstruction.

Visual thesis: a tactile miniature mechanical theater in teal iron, midnight stone, ivory and warm amber light. Elevated three-quarter perspective shows three deep cutaway rooms; the player can walk behind partial walls. Copper trims, local lamps and a clock mechanism establish scale. World fills the viewport; one compact notebook and a narrow timeline support investigation.

Content plan: immediate playable scene, a short Hebrew introduction, witness accounts near the action, editable reconstruction, then a small chapter completion overlay. Five short incidents end in a changed transfer case.

Interaction thesis: actors and brass mechanisms move as time is scrubbed; translucent trails expose only the visited room; a changed replay reveals wind and mechanical consequences. Reading pauses time. No accusation or liar mechanic. English supplies essential offscreen evidence.

Camera: elevated three-quarter with gentle player following, real perspective, depth and shadows. Silhouette: slender friendly automata and a small copper bird. Materials: painted iron, brass, stone, wood, ivory. Sound: soft clock ticks, bell, wind and recovery chime after a user gesture, with mute. Reduced motion removes trails and decorative movement.
